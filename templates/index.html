About The Project

First-Year Engineering Mini Project • Programming + IT Workshop + NSS / Community Service

The Campus Safety Incident Logger is an engineering mini-project developed to automate emergency reporting, hazard monitoring, and safety response management across KIET-1 and KIET-2 campuses.

Key Objectives

  • Eliminate slow manual paper complaint registers.
  • Enforce Roll Series validation (25B21AXXXX & 256Q1AXXXX).
  • Categorize hazards automatically using an offline AI Engine.
  • Provide real-time Chart.js analytics for campus management.

Scope & Department

  • Exclusive support for CSE Allied Branches.
  • Covers Hostel Blocks, Laboratories, Canteen, and Parking.
  • 100% offline rule-based NLP classification in Python.
  • Seamless status transition from Pending ➔ Resolved.

System Features & Capabilities

Designed with automated incident triage, real-time analytics, and preventive response workflows.

Rule-Based AI Engine

Offline Python engine analyzes keywords, risk levels, and descriptions to assign departments and SLA response times automatically.

Roll Series Validation

Enforces strict student roll verification for KIET-1 (25B21AXXXX) and KIET-2 (256Q1AXXXX).

Preventive Action Plans

Generates tailored, actionable safety measures and emergency contact recommendations for every reported incident type.

Trend Analytics Dashboard

Admin control center featuring Chart.js visual analytics, CSV/PDF exports, and status progression controls.
